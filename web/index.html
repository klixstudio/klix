How it works

Link in. Trailer out.

  1. 01Paste

    A repo or a website

    Drop in owner/repo from GitHub, or any public site like linear.app. No sign up.

  2. 02Read

    Klix reads the source

    README, stars, languages and latest release from GitHub. For a site: its copy, headings, preview image, and the colours and fonts in its CSS.

  3. 03Write

    Claude writes the cut

    A hook, feature beats and a closing line, only from what the source says. For a website it also dresses the video in the site's own look.

  4. 04Direct

    Describe or fine tune

    Tell Claude the video you want in your own words, or set it yourself: style, palette, font, music, pacing, format, effects.

  5. 05Export

    Render and post

    Your browser renders every frame into an MP4 with sound, up to 4K at 120 fps. Share to X in one tap, or copy the post text.

    Why Klix exists

    Make every launch as watchable
    as the work behind it.

    That is the whole mission. Klix is here so that the person who spent months building something does not lose its moment to a plain link nobody stops for.

    01The problem

    Great work, invisible launch

    Most launches on X are a link and two lines of text. In a feed that moves this fast, that is easy to scroll past, no matter how good the product is.

    02The gap

    Video is the format, but it is slow to make

    A proper launch video means a script, an editor, motion design and music. Big companies have a team for that. Solo builders and small teams usually do not have the hours.

    03Our goal

    A premiere in a minute, for anyone

    Paste the link you already have and get a trailer that looks and sounds like a studio made it, written from your own words, in your own colours.

    StoryWhy we built it

    We kept seeing the same thing: someone ships something careful and useful after months of work, then announces it with a link and two lines of text. It scrolls past in a second. The work was good; the launch was simply not built to be seen.

    So we built the tool we wanted for our own releases. It reads what you already wrote, your README or your website, lets Claude write and direct the cut, and renders it on your own machine. No editor to learn, no stock footage, no music to license, and nothing made up along the way.

    What can I make a video from?

    Any public GitHub repo, or any public website whose text is in the page. Sites that only show content after heavy JavaScript runs may not have enough text to read; Klix tells you when that happens.

    Does Claude make things up?

    It is told not to: lines come from your README or your site, and repo numbers come straight from GitHub. You can still edit every word before you export.

    How does website mode match my brand?

    Klix reads your site's CSS: colour tokens, the theme colour and the colours used most, plus the fonts. Claude picks the background, text and accent colours from those, and if your font is on Google Fonts the video uses it. Your preview image becomes the product shot.

    What does Describe do?

    You write the video you want in plain words. Claude sets the style, palette, font, music, pacing, format, frame rate and effects, and rewrites the script only when you ask for different words.

    Where does the music come from?

    It is not sampled from anyone. Each track is synthesized in your browser for your cut, so there is nothing to license.

    Which formats and sizes can I export?

    MP4 in square, portrait, vertical or landscape, from 720p up to 4K, at 60, 90 or 120 fps, with AAC sound. Chrome and Edge render frame by frame for exact frame rates.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Paste a link and go. There is a small daily limit on new scripts per visitor; anything already made loads again, and editing by hand is unlimited.

    Is there a watermark?

    Yes. Every video carries a small "made with klix" mark in a corner.
